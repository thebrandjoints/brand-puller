Foundation & Legacy

Built to turn bold ideas into physical reality.

Established in 2011 as Consumer Access, today Brand Puller (Private) Limited operates as a full-service marketing, fabrication, and spatial execution house based in Karachi, Pakistan.

We bring together industrial designers, structural fabricators, and event operations experts to build live brand environments grounded in transparency, exact material specifications, and strict deadline delivery.
